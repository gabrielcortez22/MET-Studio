About MET Studio

Meet the team →

MET Studio designs and builds private homes in South Florida. We draw the plans, write the finish schedule, and run the site ourselves, so the details in the render are the details on the wall.

Architecture

Project Management

For Designers and Contractors

We provide Construction Management for Designers, Architects and Contractors. We are on site 100% of the time, as the single point of coordination between the Client and the trades, so the approved design is what gets built.

Project Management

From preconstruction to turnover: scope, budgets, schedules, procurement and subcontractor buyout, then daily coordination of the trades, cost tracking and change orders.

Construction Administration

Submittals, RFIs, change orders, schedule and cost control within a design-build model, with one point of accountability.

Quality Control

Material and submittal review, mockup approvals, inspections at key stages and system testing, through to the final punch list.

Layout & Millwork Design

Space plans and custom millwork with full shop drawings for cabinetry, closets, wall paneling and built-ins, coordinated with finishes, appliances and lighting.

MEP Coordination

MEP specifications prepared with vendors and subcontractors, so every installation is done right the first time.

Experience

Up to 30 subcontractors coordinated at once on projects from $1.2MM to $35MM.

Design Consulting

We offer Design Consultation for Owners, Designers and Builders. We help choose every fixture, finish and piece of furniture so it looks right, works right and fits each project.

Plumbing Design Consultation

Fixture selection, finishes and layouts for kitchens and baths, matched to the design and to how each space will be used.

Electrical & Lighting Design Consultation

Lighting layouts, fixture selection, switching and outlet placement planned around the architecture and the furniture.

Wall Finishes Design Consultation

Guidance in choosing wall finishes, from plaster and paint to stone, wood and paneling, with samples and options presented for each space.

Furniture Selection

An experienced eye for choosing the right pieces for each project, balancing scale, materials and comfort so every room feels complete.

Technical Coordination

Specifications reviewed with suppliers and installers so rough-in locations and dimensions are right before walls are closed.

Concept to Installation

Support from the first selections through final installation, so the finished result matches what was approved.

Who we are

MET Studio brings Architects, Designers and Construction Management under one roof. The same team draws the plans, renders the spaces and runs the site, from the first sketch to the last finish.

Gabriel Cortez

Senior Architect & Interior Designer

Architecture & Project Management

A graduate of the Faculty of Architecture at the Central University of Venezuela in Caracas, Gabriel has a deep background in Architecture and Urban Planning and has focused on Residential Architecture and Construction for the last 20 years, with extensive experience in large-scale millwork manufacturing and installation for some of the most prestigious real estate developers.

He bridges Design and Construction, guiding each project from early planning to final handover as the single point of contact between client and builders, and makes sure every detail is built as designed, on schedule and to the highest standard.

gabriel@metstudio.us

Nathalia Galindo

Senior Architect & Interior Designer

Interiors & Materials Selection

Nathalia is an Architectural Designer with over six years of experience shaping residential and commercial spaces, with a focus on interiors. Raised in Caracas, Venezuela, she creates spaces rich in texture and character, where beauty and function work together.

She leads Space Planning, Material Selection and Design Development, working closely with vendors and builders so every space is carefully resolved from concept to completion.

nathalia@metstudio.us

Damian Castillo

He specializes in MEP Pre-Conceptualization and Pre-Construction, planning mechanical, electrical and plumbing systems from the earliest design stages so every project moves into construction well coordinated and ready to build.
